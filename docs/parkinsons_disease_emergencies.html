Parkinson’s disease emergencies

1 Acute Psychosis in Parkinson’s Disease

1.1 Diagnosis

Exclude secondary causes including infection (UTI, PNA, etc.), metabolic derangements, medications.

1.2 Treatment

  1. Treat secondary cause if one exists.
  2. Gradually reduce exacerbating PD medications (amantadine > dopamine agonists > COMT/MAOI > levodopa)
  3. Cautious symptomatic treatment of psychosis with the least D2 blocking agents [quetiapine > clozapine].

2 Parkinsonism-Hyperpyrexia Syndrome

2.1 Signs and symptoms

Indistinguishable from Neuroleptic Malignant Syndrome and often due to abrupt w/d of dopaminergic agents.

2.2 Diagnosis

Exclude other etiologies: infection, metabolic derangements, medications (esp. dopamine blockers).

2.3 Treatment

  1. Restart dopaminergic agents.
  2. If no PO access, establish PO access with temporary gastric tube (Nasogastric, Duotube).
    • OK to crush Sinemet IR. May need to temporarily replace Sinemet CR with IR.
      (dose of IR is approx. ½ of CR dose)
  3. If unable to use gut, may temporarily use apomorphine SC if available and only if necessary.
    • Starting dose 2 mg SC, max 5 doses per day; pretreatment with trimethobenzamide to avoid nausea.
