Antiseizure Medications (ASMs)

1 General Precautions

All antiseizure medications (ASMs) confer an elevated risk of:

  • Suicidal ideation and behavior

  • Teratogenesis

2 Pregnancy Considerations

  • Women becoming pregnant while taking ASMs should enroll in the North American Antiepileptic Drug Pregnancy Registry.
  • Registry phone: 1-888-233-2334
  • Registry website: https://www.aedpregnancyregistry.org/

2.1 Folic Acid Recommendations

Women of childbearing potential should take:

  • Folic acid 1–2 mg daily preconceptually
  • OR 1 mg daily plus prenatal vitamin

3 Adverse Event Reporting

Report ASM adverse events to the FDA MedWatch program:

https://www.fda.gov/safety/medwatch-fda-safety-information-and-adverse-event-reporting-program

3.1 Special Prescribing Considerations

Medications requiring REMS or special documentation
(therefore do not issue outpatient prescriptions without discussion with the epilepsy attending):

  • Felbamate
  • Vigabatrin
  • Fenfluramine
  • Epidiolex (not scheduled but must be dispensed from a specialty pharmacy)
