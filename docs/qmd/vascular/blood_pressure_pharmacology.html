Blood Pressure Pharmacology

1 Blood Pressure Goals

Situation Goal Notes
Ischemic stroke <220/120 Adjust goals for clinical picture
s/p tPA <180/105 Day 1–2 SBP <180, Day 3+ SBP <140 (example)
s/p thrombectomy <180/105 or per attending
Hemorrhagic <140/105 Ultimately goal SBP <140, but may tolerate slightly higher in perfusion-dependent patient with baseline >200

Agent Route Dose Tips / titration
Lisinopril PO 2.5–40 mg QD Consider Q12H dosing for additional BP lowering. If renal dysfunction: trial captopril before lisinopril to monitor Cr
Captopril PO 12.5–150 mg Q8H If already using 100 mg Q8H, already exceeds max dose lisinopril, so consider switching over to lisinopril (5:1 ratio) and add another agent
Enalaprilat IV 1.25–2.5 mg Q3 PRN PRN for uncontrolled HTN
Agent Route Dose Tips / titration
Metoprolol IV or PO (type dependent) Tartrate (IR): Q6H–Q8H
Succinate (ER): Q12H — Cannot give through feeding tube
PO → IV conversion is 2.5:1
Labetalol IV 10–60 mg PRN 10 mg IV, repeat q15 min until effective, increasing to 20 mg → 40 mg → 60 mg (max bolus dose)
Max total dose: 300 mg in 2 hours
If IPH & BP >230/120, skip to 20 mg
If unresponsive after 3 doses → nicardipine
IV requires telemetry if on the floor
PO 100–800 mg Q8H
Carvedilol PO 6.25–25 mg Q12H Has both alpha and beta blocking effects
Agent Route Dose Tips / titration
Amlodipine PO 2.5–10 mg QD Best to minimize BP variability: onset gradual in 24–72 hours and reaches steady state in 1–1.5 weeks
Nicardipine IV gtt (titrated) Refractory BP, requires ICU. Start 5 mg/hr IV, titrate by 2.5 mg/hr q15 min to max 15 mg/hr
Agent Route Dose Tips
Chlorthalidone PO 12.5–25 mg QD Twice as potent as HCTZ and longer acting. Can cause hyponatremia and increased Cr (avoid in ICU)
Agent Route Dose Tips
Clonidine PO 0.1–0.3 mg Q8H Does not increase ICP
Nitroprusside IV (titrated) 0.5 mcg/kg/min, max 10 mcg/kg/min. Can increase ICP, but consider if refractory to nicardipine
Hydralazine — — AVOID due to risk of increased ICP
Miscellaneous Tips and Hints
  • Ibuprofen & ASA: Ibuprofen should be taken >8 hrs before or 4 hours after immediate-release ASA as it binds receptors for ASA
  • SQH: Delay for 24 hours after tPA, 48 hours after IPH
    • If weight <50 kg or low BMI: SQH 5,000 Q12H
    • If BMI ≥40: enoxaparin 40 mg SQ Q12H
    • If platelet decrease 50% or greater in few days, consider HIT
  • Avoid dextrose/glucose containing IV solutions or standing orders for hypotonic fluids (even if on insulin gtt) in stroke patients
  • Clopidogrel activated through P450 2C19: fluoxetine, fluconazole, omeprazole (weak) inhibit formation of active agent
  • NOACs: apixaban is best choice in stroke patients (least IPH, BID dosing)
    • Apixaban adjusted dosing: needs to meet 2 out of 3 factors: Age ≥80 y/o, Weight <60 kg, SCr >1.5
    • Apixaban has direct drug interaction with amiodarone, but still reduced rate of stroke and major bleeding vs. warfarin
    • Not effective in antiphospholipid syndrome or valvular disease
    • Caution in enzyme inducers/inhibitors or PgP inducers/inhibitors (PHT, CBZ, St. John’s wort, rifampin, amiodarone)
