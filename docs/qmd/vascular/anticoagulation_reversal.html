Anticoagulation Reversal

  • Warfarin
  • Unfractionated Heparin
  • LMWH
  • Factor Xa Inhibitors
  • Dabigatran

Warfarin

Reversal Comments
Vitamin K 10 mg IV over 15–30 min
AND Prothrombin Complex Concentrates (PCC)

• 25 units/kg if INR 1.7–3.9
• 35 units/kg if INR 4–6
• 50 units/kg if INR >6
Max dose 5000 units		 FFP is not recommended with use of PCC unless otherwise indicated

Use FEIBA in patients with a documented heparin allergy as PCCs contain a small amount of heparin
• 500 units for INR ≤5
• 1000 units for INR >5

Unfractionated Heparin

Reversal Comments
• 1 mg Protamine IV for each 100 units of UFH. Need to consider timing and total amount of UH administered over the last 3 hours in calculation (see next column).
• Infusion should not exceed 5 mg/minute due to risk of anaphylaxis.
• Maximum single dose of Protamine is 50 mg, but can be repeated		 Following a single bolus of heparin:
a. Immediately: Dose 1 mg protamine IV for every 100 units UFH
b. 30 min to 1 hour after a bolus: Dose 0.5 mg protamine IV for every 100 units UFH
c. >2 hours after a bolus: Dose 0.25–0.375 mg protamine IV for every 100 units UFH

For continuous infusion of heparin:
Calculate dose (1 mg protamine per 100 units UFH) for all UFH given within the last hour + one half the dose of heparin given during the preceding hour + one-quarter of the dose of heparin given in the hour prior to that.

Low molecular weight Heparin (Enoxaparin, Dalteparin)

Reversal Comments
• 1 mg Protamine IV for each 1 mg of Enoxaparin in last 8 hours
• 1 mg Protamine IV for each 100 units of Dalteparin or Tinzaparin in last 8 hours
• For either regimen, may consider Factor VIIa 45–90 mcg/kg IV for life-threatening intractable bleed** (Round rVIIa dose to vial size)		 • Protamine only partially reverses the activity of LMWH
• Note differences in dosing between enoxaparin and other LMWHs
• May administer 0.5 mg protamine for every 1 mg enoxaparin (or 100 units of dalteparin) if LMWH dose administered 8–12 hours prior
• Round Factor VIIa dose to nearest 1 mg.

Rivaroxaban, Apixaban, Edoxaban

Reversal Comments
Send STAT UFH (anti-Xa level).
If < 0.6, therapeutic anticoagulation is not likely; consider no reversal.
If > 0.6: PCC 50 IU/kg IV

Do not recheck UFH level after administration of PCC as value will not change and is not indicative of clinical hemostasis.
Do not check UFH for edoxaban.		 Do not redose PCC unless clinical failure.
Alternative if documented heparin allergy (i.e., cannot receive PCC): Factor VIIa 45–90 mcg/kg (round to nearest 1 mg) or FEIBA 50 units/kg

Note: Andexanet alfa did not receive full FDA approval and has been removed from the UF formulary

Dabigatran

Reversal Comments
Note: If thrombin time and aPTT are normal there is likely no anticoagulant effect and “reversal” is not necessary

• Activated oral charcoal if ingestion occurred within 2 hours
• Idarucizumab 5 gm IV once		 • Consider HD, especially if renal impairment
• If no clinical response to idarucizumab, consider Prothrombin Complex Concentrates (PCC) 50 units/kg**
OR
FEIBA 50 units/kg may be tried if no clinical response to PCC or documented heparin allergy
• Note: If thrombin time and aPTT are normal there is likely no anticoagulant effect and “reversal” is not necessary

Tenecteplase

Reversal Comments
Cryoprecipitate 10 units IV PLUS
Tranexamic acid 1 gm IV once
Obtain TEG and fibrinogen level 2 hr after reversal to assess need for further therapy		 If bleeding is refractory or life threatening consider aminocaproic acid 0.1 g/kg bolus in addition to cryoprecipitate and tranexamic acid

Antiplatelets

Agent Reversal Comments
Enteric-Coated Aspirin Best supportive care (based on PATCH trial)
or 1 unit single donor platelet if a surgical procedure is scheduled or bleeding is refractory (*d/w stroke attg)		 Can consider repeat 1 single donor unit if PFA-100 (orderable in EPIC) is not normalized.
Consider DDAVP 0.3 mcg/kg or rVIIa 45–90 mcg/kg (round to nearest 1 mg) if refractory bleeding
Clopidogrel (Plavix®) Best supportive care (based on PATCH trial)
or 2 units of single donor platelets if a surgical procedure is scheduled or bleeding is refractory (*d/w stroke attg)		 Consider repeating 2 units Q12 hours until the PFA-100 and Verify Now (Plavix inhibition <20% or PRU over 240) assays are normal. Both tests are orderable in EPIC. Consider DDAVP iv or rVIIa IV if refractory bleeding
Note

Per P&T committee guidelines adult hematology consult required for approval of Factor VIIa / rVIIa.
