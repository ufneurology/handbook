1 Neuroleptic Malignant Syndrome (NMS)

1.1 Signs and symptoms

  1. Mental status change: agitated delirium, confusion, encephalopathy (82%)
  2. Muscular rigidity: “lead pipe” tremor (up to 92%) → elevated CPK and myoglobinuric acute renal failure
  3. Hyperthermia: >38°C (87%), >40°C (40%)
  4. Autonomic instability: tachycardia (88%), labile BP (77%), tachypnea (73%), diaphoresis

1.2 Diagnosis

Exclude other etiologies: acute drug intoxication, metabolic derangements, meningitis, etc.

1.3 Treatment

  1. Admit to ICU
  2. Discontinue offending agent (e.g. neuroleptics, D2 blocking antiemetics or abrupt w/d levodopa)
  3. Supportive care for Autonomic instability, DVT/PE prophylaxis, IVFs to prevent rhabdo
  4. Symptomatic treatment:
    • Agitation — Lorazepam 0.5–1 mg prn (may also help with rigidity)
    • Rigidity — Dantrolene 0.25–2 mg/kg IV q6–12 hours (max dose 10 mg/kg/day; be aware of hepatotoxicity)
    • Fever — cooling blankets, ice lavage, acetaminophen

2 Serotonin Syndrome (SS)

2.1 Signs and symptoms

  1. Mental status change: agitated delirium, restlessness, disorientation
  2. Neuromuscular hyperreactivity: tremor, rigidity, myoclonus, hyperreflexia (+Babinski and clonus)
  3. Autonomic instability: dilated pupils, diaphoresis, hypertension, diarrhea, vomiting

2.2 Diagnosis

Exclude other etiologies: acute drug intoxication, metabolic derangements, meningitis, etc.

2.3 Treatment

  1. Admit to ICU
  2. Discontinue offending agent (e.g. SSRIs, MAOIs, buspirone, trazodone, lithium, opiates, etc.)
  3. Supportive care for Autonomic instability, DVT/PE prophylaxis
  4. Symptomatic treatment:
    • Agitation, Neuromuscular hyperreactivity — Lorazepam 0.5–1 mg prn
    • Cyproheptadine (only for severe cases) 12 mg PO, then 2 mg q2 hours until clinical response

3 Acute Dystonic Reaction

3.1 Signs and symptoms

Intermittent or sustained dystonic posturing due to blockade of D2 receptor (neuroleptics, certain antiemetics)

3.2 Diagnosis

  1. Review medication list/MAR for offending agent.
  2. Exclude metabolic derangements (hypomagnesemia, hypocalcemia) or other drug toxicity.

3.3 Treatment

  1. Discontinue offending agent
  2. Diphenhydramine 25–50 mg IV/IM vs Lorazepam 0.5–1 mg IV (acute); Diphenhydramine 25 mg PO qid (×48 hr)
  3. Alternative therapy: Benztropine 2 mg IV/IM (acute); Benztropine 1–2 mg PO bid (×48 hr)
