IV DHE Protocol

Dihydroergotamine (DHE) is a serotonin agonist with neurologic effects. It is also a venoconstrictor; those with difficult IV access may require PICC line. DHE can be used to treat refractory migraine or cluster headache.

Important

Counsel patient that when DHE protocol is complete that unfortunately there are no other inpatient therapies and discharge will occur even if headache hasn’t improved.

0.1 Contraindications

  • Peripheral vascular disease
  • CAD
  • Stroke
  • Severe or poorly controlled HTN
  • Liver or renal impairment
  • Pregnancy
  • Triptan use within 24 hours

1 PROTOCOL

  1. Admit to Neurology/telemetry floor bed, START IVF NS @ 75 cc/hr; continue home preventative agents
  2. Baseline EKG, weight, CBC, CMP, Mag, Phos, pregnancy urine screen
  3. Give one-time Depakote 500 mg and Dexamethasone 8 mg PO
  4. Order DHE 0.5 mg IV ×1, followed by 0.5–1 mg q8h (9–10 doses)
  5. Premedicate with Benadryl 25 mg IV + Phenergan 25 mg IV
  6. Measure vitals before each infusion. Hold if SBP > 170 or DBP > 100
  7. Can slow rate of infusion per patient preference
  8. Monitor for common side effects: chest pain (stop infusion, EKG, trops), GI symptoms, diarrhea
  9. Can order scheduled IV Mag 2 g qd, Toradol 15–30 mg q8h prn for breakthrough pain
Note

If headache has completely resolved, treat for an additional 3 doses and then consider early discontinuation.
