Common Agents Affecting Neurologic Exam

1 Barbiturates

Drug Half Life (t½) Distribution Suggested time to brain death exam
Pentobarbital ~4-50 hrs ~35-45% bound to plasma proteins Pentobarbital level ≤ 10 mcg/ml
Phenobarbital ~2-6 days ~20-45% bound to plasma proteins Phenobarbital level ≤ 10 mcg/ml

2 Benzodiazepines

Drug Half Life (t½) Distribution Suggested time to brain death exam
Lorazepam 10-20 hrs Widely in most body tissues —
Midazolam 1-12.5 hrs Widely, 95% protein bound —
Diazepam 20-50 hrs, active metabolite 40-100 hrs Widely, 94-99% protein bound Variable, if expect toxicity, wait 1.5-2 times the half life
Chlordiazepoxide 10-48 hrs, active metabolite 14-95 hrs Widely —

3 Neuromuscular Blocking Agents

Drug Half Life (t½) Distribution Suggested time to brain death exam
Atracurium Biphasic t½ (2-3.4 min and 20 min respectively) Low body distribution —
Pancuronium Triphasic t½, terminal t½ ~2hr — —
Rocuronium Triphasic t½, terminal t½ ~2hr 87% protein bound —
Succinylcholine Metabolized rapidly, with effect lasting ~10 min Widely Train of four via peripheral nerve stimulation should result in four thumb twitches
Vecuronium Biphasic t½ (3-9 min and 30-80 min respectively) Not fully characterized —

4 Narcotics

Drug Half Life (t½) Distribution Suggested time to brain death exam
Morphine 1.5-4.5 hrs Widely, 30-35% protein bound —
Fentanyl 3-7 hrs, duration 30-60 mins Widely, 80-86% protein bound —
Hydromorphone 2.5 hrs, duration 4-5 hrs Two distributive t½, 1.27 and 14.7 hrs respectively —
Meperidine 3.2-3.7 hrs, active metabolite 24-48 hrs Widely, 65-80% protein bound —
Oxycodone N/A N/A N/A

5 Miscellaneous Drugs

Drug Half Life (t½) Distribution Suggested time to brain death exam
Propofol Biphasic: alpha t½ = 1-8 mins, beta t½ = 1.5-12.4 hrs Moderately Brain death exam after 2-3 times half life. Neuro exam ok after drip off for 5-10 mins
Dopamine ~2 minutes, duration ~10 minutes — Extremely high doses can fix/dilate pupils
Atropine Duration of cardiac effects ~5 mins — Pupil effects after IV are transient
Ketamine 2-3 hrs 50% protein bound Recovery time 2-3 hrs
