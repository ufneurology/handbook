Common Critical Care Pharmacology

1 Sedatives

Drug Dose Adverse Reactions Pearls
Propofol
GABAa receptor agonist		 Max 50 mcg/kg/min Hypotension, Apnea, Movement, Pain at injection site, Hypertriglyceridemia, Pancreatitis, PRIS (hyperkalemia, dysrhythmia, lipemia, metabolic acidosis, heart failure, rhabdomyolysis) Contraindications: Allergy to soy or egg. Use with caution in cardiovascular disease. Rapid onset/offset. Lipid vehicle susceptible to bacterial contamination — change IV tubing/bottle q12h. Blue-green urine discoloration. Delivers 1.1 kcal/ml.
Dexmedetomidine
Alpha-2 agonist		 Maintenance: 0.2-1.4 mcg/kg/h
Loading dose (1 mcg/kg) NOT recommended		 Hypotension, Transient hypotension, severe bradycardia, sinus arrest during loading dose, Bradycardia Has analgesic properties. Goal is patient arousable with stimulation and sedative state when stimulation removed. Does not require mechanical ventilation.
Lorazepam
GABAa receptor agonist		 Loading: 0.02-0.04 mg/kg
Intermittent: 0.02-0.06 mg/kg q2-6 h (max 2 mg IV for agitation at 2 mg/min)
Maintenance infusion: 0.01-0.1 mg/kg/h		 Hypotension, Respiratory depression, Drowsiness, Pain at injection site, Akathisia, Confusion, Anterograde amnesia, Visual disturbances, Paradoxical reactions Precipitation and metabolic acidosis possible; inline filter recommended for infusions.
Midazolam
GABAa receptor agonist		 Loading: 0.01-0.05 mg/kg
Maintenance: 0.01-0.1 mg/kg/h		 Similar to lorazepam Rapid onset and short duration. Active metabolites may accumulate in renal dysfunction.

2 Opioids

Drug Usual dose Adverse Reactions Pearls
Fentanyl Bolus: 50-100 mcg or 1-2 mcg/kg IV
Maintenance: 0.7-10 mcg/kg/hr or 25-700 mcg/h		 Respiratory depression, Bradycardia, Edema, Confusion, Sedation, Mood changes, Constipation, Miosis, Chest wall rigidity, Diaphoresis, Myoclonus May be alternative in morphine allergy. Do not use transdermal in acute setting (delayed onset 6-24 h).
Hydromorphone Oral: 2-4 mg q4-6h
IV: 0.2-1 mg q4-6h		 Respiratory depression, Bradycardia, Edema, Confusion, Sedation, Mood changes, Constipation, Miosis, Diaphoresis, Myoclonus May be alternative in morphine allergy.
Morphine Bolus: 2-10 mg IVP
Intermediate: 2-8 mg q3-4h
Maintenance infusion: 0.8-30		 Itching, Respiratory depression, Sedation, Pruritus, Urinary retention, Pain at injection site, Dizziness Active metabolites accumulate in renal dysfunction — reduced dose recommended. Can induce histamine release causing itching.

Naloxone (antagonist)
- IV/IM: 0.04-0.4 mg
- Intranasal: 1-2 mg per naris (repeat q2-3 min, switch to IV/IM)
Adverse: Tachycardia, Hypertension, Nausea, Vomiting, Tremor, Diaphoresis, Dyspnea, Withdrawal symptoms.
Pearl: Short half-life — effects may wear off before opioid cleared (renarcotization risk).

3 Neuromuscular Blocking Agents

Agent Dosing Onset Duration Pearls Adverse Effects
Succinylcholine Adults: 0.5-1.1 mg/kg IV
IM: 2-4 mg/kg		 Very Rapid (IV 30-60s) Short (3-5 min, max 10 min) Cannot be reversed. Contraindicated in malignant hyperthermia, hyperkalemia risk. Hypersensitivity, Anaphylaxis, Cardiac arrest, Hyperkalemia, Rhabdomyolysis
Vecuronium 0.1 mg/kg IV (up to 0.2 mg/kg) Prolonged (3-9 min) Intermediate (35-45 min) No significant CV effects. No effect on ICP. Malignant hyperthermia, Hypertension, Hyperkalemia
Cisatracurium 0.15-0.2 mg/kg IV Fast (1.5-2 min) Prolonged (45-75 min adults) Hofmann elimination — good in renal/hepatic failure. Prolonged respiratory depression, Jaw rigidity
Rocuronium 0.6-1.2 mg/kg IV Rapid (1-2 min) Intermediate (20-35 min) Prolonged in renal failure. Rhabdomyolysis, Myalgias, Skeletal muscle weakness

4 Vasopressors / Inotropes

Vasopressor/Inotrope Adrenergic receptor activation Initial dosing Indications Advantages Disadvantages
Norepinephrine α1, β1 2-5 mcg/min OR 0.02-0.06 mcg/kg/min Septic shock with low SVR Great for increasing SVR/MAP while preserving CO. First line for septic, cardiogenic, hypovolemic shock. May increase O2 consumption, Myocardial ischemia, Decrease intestinal perfusion
Dopamine D1 (0-5), β1 (5-10), α1 (10-15) 0-15 mcg/kg/min Poor cardiac function, Hypotension with bradycardia Effective at multiple receptors Highest risk of dysrhythmias at high doses
Epinephrine β1, α1, β2 0.02-0.05 mcg/kg/min Septic shock with low SVR/CO, Anaphylactic shock First line for septic shock Risk of dysrhythmias, Myocardial ischemia
Phenylephrine α1 10-200 mcg/min OR 0.1-1 mcg/kg/min Rapid MAP increases post-sedation No β effects, less arrhythmogenic May decrease CO, Reflex bradycardia
Vasopressin 0 0.02-0.04 units/min Refractory hypotension in septic shock Spares pulmonary vasoconstriction May decrease CO in CAD
Dobutamine β1, α1, β2 2.5-10 mcg/kg/min Acute decompensated HF Improves CO in decompensated HF Can decrease SVR, Provoke hypotension
